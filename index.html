Spatial Harp

Free Virtual Harp Online — AR Webcam Musical Instrument

Play glowing 3D strings in mid-air with your hands using just your webcam — no download or VR headset needed. Switch between Plucked Harp, Laser Synth, Ambient Chime, and Retro 8-Bit presets across five musical scales.

  1. Pinch & drag: Hold steady until it pops, then draw a string.
  2. Swipe to pluck: Cross any string with your fingertips to play.
  3. Grab anchors: Pinch tips with one or both hands to resize or tune.

Camera processing runs locally in your browser — never recorded or uploaded.
